The evidence report
> every finding, quote-anchored, on one page

Findings sit beside the passage they came from.

Real DOI resolves. Fabricated one doesn't. Cited quote isn't in the source paper. Pangram score: 0.96.

Under the rail: an "All checks" ledger — every registered check runs, gaps report themselves. No LLM prose in the evidence layer.
