# Hartwell Foundation
## Education Innovation Grants — Request for Proposals

**Cycle:** Fall 2026
**Reference code:** HF-EIG-26F

## 1. Purpose

The Hartwell Foundation funds small, evidence-building grants that
adapt existing, classroom-tested instructional interventions for
under-resourced school settings — particularly rural districts, small
charter networks, and districts with limited access to specialist
coaching staff. We are not a general K-12 funder; we fund adaptation and
rigorous small-scale evaluation of things that have already shown
promise elsewhere, not the invention of new curricula from scratch.

## 2. Eligibility

Eligible applicants:

- Nonprofit research or teacher-education institutions (colleges of
  education, independent education-research nonprofits);
- Public school districts or regional education service agencies
  applying in partnership with a research lead.

The Principal Investigator must have prior experience running a
classroom-based intervention study (pilot or full-scale). First-time PIs
must name a co-PI or senior personnel member who meets this bar.

Any project involving data collection from students under 18 must
include appropriate human-subjects review. See Section 7.

## 3. Priority Areas

For Fall 2026, the Foundation is prioritizing proposals in:

- Elementary and middle-grades mathematics, particularly fraction and
  proportional reasoning;
- Early literacy intervention for multilingual learners;
- Adaptation of specialist-coaching-dependent interventions for
  paraprofessional or lower-touch delivery models.

Proposals outside these three areas will not be considered in this
cycle.

## 4. Award Amount and Duration

- **Maximum award:** $85,000 total.
- **Duration:** single project year (up to 12 months from award start).
- The Foundation does not fund multi-year commitments through this
  program; renewal is possible but requires a full new application.

Applications requesting more than $85,000 in total costs will be
declined without panel review.

## 5. Format and Word Limits

- Combined narrative (Abstract through Approach) must not exceed
  **3,000 words**, excluding budget, references, and attachments.
- No page-limit or font requirement is imposed; word count governs.
- Submit as a single PDF.
- Tables and figures are exempt from the word count but should be used
  sparingly; a narrative that relies on tables to circumvent the word
  limit may be returned for reformatting.

## 6. Required Sections

Use the following headings, in order:

1. Title
2. PI/Institution
3. Abstract
4. Aims
5. Background & Prior Work
6. Approach
7. Evaluation Plan — how the project will measure whether the
   adaptation worked, including the specific instrument(s) used and the
   comparison or baseline condition.
8. Budget

## 7. Required Attachments

- **IRB Approval Letter or Exempt-Determination Letter.** Any project
  collecting data from students, teachers, or classrooms must include
  either an IRB approval letter or a formal exempt-determination letter
  from the applicant institution's IRB (or a partner institution's IRB
  operating under an existing agreement). Proposals describing
  student-level data collection without this attachment will be
  considered non-compliant and will not advance to panel review.
- **Letter of Support from each partner district**, confirming
  willingness to participate and, where applicable, existing
  relationships (e.g., through a regional education service agency).
- **One-page CV** for the PI (co-PI CV also required if named per
  Section 2).

## 8. Prohibited Content

- Undisclosed AI-generated narrative presented as original applicant
  analysis.
- Citations to sources that cannot be independently verified.
- Claims of statistical significance without reporting the underlying
  sample size and effect size.
- Any request for indirect costs exceeding 10% of direct costs.

## 9. Review Criteria

Applications are scored by a review panel on evidence base for the
underlying intervention, quality and feasibility of the proposed
adaptation, strength of the evaluation design, and district partnership
readiness. The Foundation does not publish a detailed weighted rubric
for this program; panelists apply the criteria above holistically.

- Submit via the Foundation's portal: **grants.hartwellfoundation.example**.
- Late or emailed submissions are not accepted.
