## Why it's the useful one

It separates two checks that a naive tool conflates. `has_fabricated_citations`
is caught by DOI resolution alone (demo recall 1.00). `has_mismatched_citations`
has a **resolving** DOI, so resolution passes it — demo recall 0.00 on 9 positives.
Only a `metadata_match` / quote check catches it. Maps to `Finding.verdict`
`overstated` / `unsupported` in DATA_MODEL.md.

DOI but is cited as "Kim et al. (2023), PNAS". ruff clean; score.py shows the new
