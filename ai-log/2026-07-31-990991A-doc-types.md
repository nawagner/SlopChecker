# 2026-07-31 — 990991A / Alex (Claude Opus 4.8) — Fixture doc types: blog + report

**Issues worked:** #22 (stacked on `alex/22-synth-fixtures`).

## What changed

- `scripts/synth/synth_proposals.py` — added a `document_type` dimension
  (`grant_application` | `blog_post` | `think_tank_report`). Each type gets its
  own section structure (grant: aims/approach/budget; blog: dek/intro/body/
  takeaway; report: exec summary/background/findings/recommendations), title
  templates, citation heading (References / Sources / Endnotes), and an
  anthropic prompt. Defects are gated by type: `budget_inflated` and
  `missing_methods` are grant-only; `fabricated_citations` and `overclaims`
  apply to all three. `manifest.csv` and `coverage.json` gained the column.
- `tests/fixtures/synthetic/` — regenerated to span all three types (120 docs).
- READMEs updated; blog/report moved out of "open items."

## Decisions

- **Defect applicability is enforced in `_defect_flags`, not just at tuple
  build.** Verified in the smoke test: no non-grant doc ever carries
  `budget_inflated` / `missing_methods` = true.
- **Kept the committed set `--offline`** (fully fabricated) — human blog/report
  docs are fabricated regardless, since there's no public human corpus for them.
- **Reused the citation + laundering + scoring machinery unchanged** — only the
  section/prompt layer branches by type, so `score.py` needed no change (extra
  manifest column is ignored by name-keyed reads).

## Caveat worth seeing

Adding blog/report prose dropped the naive baseline's `ai_generated` recall to
~0.39 and `overclaims` to ~0.29 on the committed corpus — the grant-tuned
heuristics don't generalize. Good signal that the corpus stresses checks across
document styles, not just one.
