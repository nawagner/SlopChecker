1. Background

Community-scale clean energy can lower household costs: households in one
community solar program saw average monthly electricity bills fall by about
12 percent (Hensley & Ortiz, 2024). Urban greening delivers measurable
cooling, and planting street trees lowered peak summer surface temperatures
on the treated blocks (Underhill, 2022).

2. Health and Environment

Care-transitions work has shown promise; one program reduced 30-day hospital
readmissions by 20 percent (Marsh et al., 2023). Environmental pressures are
mounting as well, and coastal erosion rates along the Vasquez shoreline
accelerated over the past decade (Ferreira, 2022).
