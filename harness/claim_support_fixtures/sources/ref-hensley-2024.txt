We evaluated 1,410 households enrolled in the Larkfield community solar
program between 2021 and 2023. Relative to a matched comparison group,
enrolled households saw average monthly electricity bills fall by 12.3
percent (95% CI: 10.1 to 14.5 percent). The effect was stable across the
three income tiers we examined, and did not attenuate over the two-year
follow-up window.
